# Academic and Empirical Evidence: Do Social Media Signals Predict Small-Cap Crypto / Memecoin Returns?

Scope note: about 22 searches and fetches, run 2026-10-26. I read full text for Maitre/Pugachyov/Weigert (Twitter attention), Marino et al. (pump.fun success), the pump.fun survival-analysis preprint (arXiv 2607.02823), and the CoinGecko lifespan study. Other papers come from abstracts or secondary summaries, and each entry says which. Sample periods are given where known, because market structure changed a lot. Pre-2021 work covers CEX-listed altcoins and Telegram/Discord pump groups. Work from 2024 on covers launchpads such as pump.fun on Solana.

## 1. Do tweet volume, mention velocity, sentiment or attention predict short-horizon returns for small caps? Decay and reversal?

### Takeaway
Social attention goes with large returns on the same day. Over days to weeks, the best cross-sectional evidence shows those returns reversing (overreaction), not a tradable continuation. For Bitcoin, tweet counts predict volume and volatility better than returns. The reversal is hardest to trade in exactly the small caps, because they cannot be shorted, so in practice the signal works as a warning not to buy late, not as an entry signal.

### Cited Findings
- **Maitre, Pugachyov & Weigert, "Twitter-Based Attention and the Cross-Section of Cryptocurrency Returns" (working paper, Feb 2024; sample 2018-2022, 161 largest coins with Twitter accounts, daily data from CoinMarketCap without survivorship bias).** Abnormal attention, defined as the log change in daily tweet count, is positively linked to returns on the same day. Coins with high abnormal attention then earn low future returns, which the authors read as investor overreaction (Barber-Odean / Da-Engelberg-Gao channel). — [FoFI 2024 PDF](http://wp.lancs.ac.uk/fofi2024/files/2024/04/FoFI-2024-148-Nikolay-Pugachyov.pdf)
  - Effect size: a daily long-short tercile portfolio (long high attention change, short low) has a next-day alpha of **-0.08% per day**, or about **-29% annualized**, against the Liu-Tsyvinski-Wu (2022) crypto factor model. The alphas do not all share a sign across specifications, so the authors call it "more likely to be an anomaly than a new risk factor". — same source
  - The authors say the portfolios are "unlikely" to be implementable in practice because most cryptocurrencies are hard to short. — same source
  - **Small vs large caps:** the next-day reversal is statistically significant for coins above median market cap but **not for coins below median**. The authors explain this as limits to arbitrage. The predictability is somewhat stronger for tokens than for coins. Results hold before and after the early-2021 GameStop/WSB episode. — same source
  - The overreaction comes from tweets by users (ticker/mention tweets), not from projects' official accounts. Official tweets instead forecast GitHub development activity. Posting by influential users makes the overreaction bigger, and the authors conclude "even small influential users can profit from their popularity to manipulate cryptocurrency prices". — same source
  - About 90% of cryptocurrencies have Twitter accounts, against about 50% of US public firms (as cited in the paper). — same source
- **Shen, Urquhart & Wang, "Does Twitter predict Bitcoin?" (Economics Letters 174, 2019; Sep 2014 - Aug 2018).** The number of tweets significantly drives next-day trading volume and realized volatility (linear and nonlinear Granger causality tests), but there is "no significant evidence" that it predicts Bitcoin returns. — [Univ. of Reading repository](https://centaur.reading.ac.uk/80420/); [IDEAS](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/ecolet/v174y2019icp118-122.html)
- **Liu & Tsyvinski, "Risks and Returns of Cryptocurrency" (RFS 34(6), 2021).** Crypto returns are driven by factors specific to crypto. "Proxies for investor attention strongly forecast future cryptocurrency returns", together with time-series momentum. This is mostly a large-cap / aggregate-market result. — [IDEAS/RFS](https://ideas.repec.org/a/oup/rfinst/v34y2021i6p2689-2727..html); [NBER WP](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w24877/w24877.pdf)
- **Steinert & Herff, "Predicting altcoin returns using social media" (PLOS ONE, 2018).** Presented as the first rigorous test on small cryptocurrencies. Twitter activity and sentiment could predict returns "for some currencies" only, not across the board. — [PMC6279012](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6279012/)
- **Attention and reversal before and during COVID (PMC11602072).** Investor attention positively affects returns of small and mid-cap cryptocurrencies. Reversal effects matter only for small caps, and their sign flipped from before the pandemic to during it. Lesson: the regime is unstable. — [PMC11602072](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11602072/)
- **Causal preprint on social media sentiment.** The estimated causal effect of sentiment shocks is negative (contrarian): positive sentiment shocks are followed by falling returns, which the authors call peak-optimism reversal. Preprint, not peer-reviewed. — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/410033900_Causal_Effects_of_Social_Media_Sentiment_on_Cryptocurrency_Market_Returns_and_Volatility)
- **Bot contamination in microcaps.** A study of microcap manipulation through social sentiment estimates that at least 1-14% of collected tweets came from bot accounts. — [ResearchGate: Discovering Market Manipulation Through Social Media Sentiment Analysis in Microcap Cryptocurrencies](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/373103013_Discovering_Market_Manipulation_Through_Social_Media_Sentiment_Analysis_in_Microcap_Cryptocurrencies)
- **Benedetti & Kostovetsky, "Digital Tulips?" (J. Corporate Finance 66, 2021; 2,390 completed ICOs that raised $12B).** Average return from ICO price to first-day open is 179% (held about 16 days on average), and buy-and-hold abnormal returns over the first 30 trading days are 48%. The authors relate prices to Twitter activity and followers; Maitre et al. cite the paper as evidence for an overreaction channel. I did not see the Twitter-specific effect sizes. — [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=3182169); [Quantpedia summary](https://quantpedia.com/returns-to-investors-in-initial-coin-offerings/)

### Inferences
- The pattern that holds across studies: attention spikes coincide with price spikes, and the information mostly arrives at the same time or a day later. After that, attention predicts mean reversion or nothing. A social signal that is "fast" on a daily or weekly scale is already too late.
- Small caps cannot be shorted, so the overreaction is not arbitraged away there. That is why reversal is statistically weak in small caps (Maitre et al.), even though overreaction is plausibly largest there. A long-only small-cap scanner faces adverse selection: the coins that are trending are on average the ones about to underperform.
- Any edge would have to come within minutes to hours of the attention rising, before the same-day move. Daily-frequency academic studies cannot see that window.

### Gaps
- I could not reach the text of Kraaijeveld & De Smedt (2020, J. Int. Financial Markets, Institutions & Money). It was reported as finding Twitter sentiment predictive for BTC, BCH and LTC, with bots present, but I could not verify effect sizes. Phillips & Gorse (Reddit/Twitter bubble and epidemic models, about 2017-2018): no sources found in this pass.
- I found no peer-reviewed study that measures mention velocity at intraday horizons (minutes to hours) specifically for small caps or memecoins, with a decay curve.
- Liu & Tsyvinski: the abstracts do not say which attention proxies they use (Google searches vs Twitter), so I could not confirm the exact measures or coefficients.
- No study I found tests social-signal returns after realistic DEX slippage for small caps.

## 2. Telegram pump-and-dump schemes: returns to early vs late participants and predictability of target coins

### Takeaway
Telegram/Discord pump groups (2017-2020, mostly on CEXs such as Binance and Yobit) were very common: about 4,800 pumps in six months of 2018. They distort prices by about 65% on average, and the loss falls on the participants who arrive late. Before the pump, target coins can be predicted from market data (small cap, low rank, illiquid exchanges). Once a pump starts, it can be detected within seconds.

### Cited Findings
- **Hamrick, Rouhi, Mukherjee, Feder, Gandal, Moore & Vasek, "The Economics of Cryptocurrency Pump and Dump Schemes" (CEPR DP13404 / WEIS 2019; mid-Jan to early Jul 2018).** The authors joined the groups and found **4,818 pump signals (3,767 on Telegram, 1,051 on Discord) across 300+ coins**. They call the practice "widespread and often quite profitable". Coins with a lower CoinMarketCap rank had bigger pump price moves. — [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=3310307); [WEIS PDF](https://weis2019.econinfosec.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/6/2019/05/WEIS_2019_paper_14.pdf); [VoxEU](https://voxeu.org/publications/dp13404); rank finding via [Breaker](https://breakermag.com/study-finds-4818-pump-and-dump-schemes-on-discord-and-telegram/)
- **Dhawan & Putniņš, "A New Wolf in Town? Pump-and-Dump Manipulation in Cryptocurrency Markets" (Review of Finance 27(3), 2023; 355 pumps in about 6 months, Binance and Yobit).** Pumps cause average price distortions of **65%**, abnormal volume in the millions of dollars, and large wealth transfers. Crypto organizers announce the target openly, so there is no misinformation; most participants other than the organizers lose money. People keep joining despite negative expected returns, which the authors explain with overconfidence and gambling preferences, and they find empirical support for both. — [EBSCO abstract](https://www.ebsco.com/articles/business-and-management/b2bb78b5-1c72-5082-bb5c-ac3c0a5c92dd/new-wolf-in-town-pump-and-dump-manipulation-in-cryptocurrency-markets); [IIMB](https://research.iimb.ac.in/fac_pubs/250); [UTS draft](https://opus.cloud1.lib.uts.edu.au/bitstream/10453/145498/2/Pump%20and%20dump%20paper%20draft%20v6.8.pdf)
- **Xu & Livshits, "The Anatomy of a Cryptocurrency Pump-and-Dump Scheme" (USENIX Security 2019; 412 Telegram pumps, Jun 2018 - Feb 2019).** A machine-learning model predicts which coin will be pumped before the announcement. A trading strategy based on it returned **up to 60% over about 2.5 months** on small retail-sized positions. The earlier arXiv preprint (220 pumps, Jul-Nov 2018) reported up to 80% in 3 weeks; cite the published 60%. — [USENIX](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity19/presentation/xu-jiahua); [arXiv 1811.10109](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.10109v1)
- **La Morgia, Mei, Sassi & Stefa, "The Doge of Wall Street: Analysis and Detection of Pump and Dump Cryptocurrency Manipulations" (ACM TOIT 23(1), 2023; >3 years of monitoring Telegram/Discord groups, about 900 events).** A machine-learning detector flags a pump **within 25 seconds of its start, with 94.5% F1**. The paper also covers Reddit "crowd pumps" (GME, DOGE/XRP). Earlier version: ICCCN 2020, "Pump and Dumps in the Bitcoin Era". — [arXiv 2105.00733](https://export.arxiv.org/abs/2105.00733); [ICCCN 2020 version](https://arxiv.org/html/2005.06610v2); [Sapienza IRIS](https://iris.uniroma1.it/handle/11573/1673225)
- **Kamps & Kleinberg, "To the moon: defining and detecting cryptocurrency pump-and-dumps" (Crime Science 7:18, 2018).** Proposes criteria for defining crypto pumps and detects suspected pumps through anomalies in price and volume. Pumps cluster on specific exchanges and coins. Evaluation used a small sample of manually identified pump signals. — [Crime Science PDF](https://crimesciencejournal.biomedcentral.com/track/pdf/10.1186/s40163-018-0093-5); [UCL Discovery](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10069142/); [BMC blog](https://blogs.biomedcentral.com/on-society/2019/01/22/cryptocurrency-pump-and-dumps/)

### Inferences
- The structure guarantees losses for late buyers. Organizers and insiders buy before the signal; the public buys at the announcement and dumps within minutes. A social-media scanner that "finds" a coin when its Telegram/X mentions spike is almost by construction one of the late participants.
- Two windows are documented as predictable. Before the pump, target selection follows low market cap, low rank and particular exchanges (Xu & Livshits). After the start, detection takes seconds (La Morgia). The first is the only one that is profitable, and it depends on CEX-era specifics (Binance/Yobit pairs, BTC-denominated pumps) that may not carry over to DEX memecoins.
- Results from 2018-2020 come from a market dominated by CEX pump groups. Post-2024 manipulation has largely moved on-chain to launchpads (Section 4), where insider "bundling" and sniping replace the announced pump.

### Gaps
- None of the sources I accessed gave precise per-pump split returns for early vs late participants (e.g., P&L by entry second). Dhawan & Putniņš say most non-organizers lose, but I did not get their exact numbers.
- Precision/recall of the Xu & Livshits target-prediction model: not retrieved, only the strategy return.

## 3. Influencer / KOL effects: Musk-Dogecoin and paid or shilled promotions

### Takeaway
Influencer tweets cause a positive price jump within hours or a day, then negative cumulative returns over 10-90 days. The decline is worse for smaller-cap tokens and for influencers with larger followings. The Musk effect on DOGE was large intraday (high single to double digits within minutes to hours) and mostly limited to Dogecoin, not Bitcoin.

### Cited Findings
- **Merkley, Pacelli, Piorkowski & Williams, "Crypto-Influencers" (Review of Accounting Studies 29(3), 2024, pp. 2254-2297; about 36,000 tweets by 180 prominent influencers on 1,600+ crypto assets, through Dec 2022).** Tweets raise prices at first, but the gains reverse. Average cumulative returns are negative at 10, 30 and 90 days after a tweet, and **worse for smaller-cap tokens**. Effects are strongest for self-described experts and accounts with large followings. — [IDEAS/RePEc](https://ideas.repec.org/a/spr/reaccs/v29y2024i3d10.1007_s11142-024-09838-4.html); [Oxford Business Law Blog](https://blogs.law.ox.ac.uk/oblb/blog-post/2023/11/crypto-influencers-give-poor-investment-advice-and-sec-taking-notice); [Kelley School blog](https://blog.kelley.iu.edu/?p=4898)
  - Secondary summaries report about **+1.83% the day after a tweet, -2.24% at 10 days, -6.53% at 30 days**. These come from a secondary source and have not been checked against the paper's tables. — [Futurity](https://www.futurity.org/crypto-influencer-advice-investment-3260122)
  - Co-author Merkley says the early gain fits attention-grabbing and the later decline fits pump-and-dump behavior. — [Kelley blog](https://blog.kelley.iu.edu/?p=4898). Jagolinzer's discussion in the same issue offers social-identity motives as another explanation for why followers participate. — [SUFE listing](https://academicnewsletter.sufe.edu.cn/info/413908)
- **Ante, "How Elon Musk's Twitter activity moves cryptocurrency markets" (Technological Forecasting & Social Change 186, 2023; 47 crypto-related Musk tweet events).** Significant positive abnormal returns and volume follow the events, but price effects are significant on average **only for Dogecoin-related tweets, not Bitcoin**. In the earlier version (6 events), "One word: Doge" produced an **8.17% cumulative abnormal return within 5 minutes, peaking at 17.31% within 1 hour**. — [IDEAS](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/tefoso/v186y2023ipas0040162522006333.html); [Blockchain Research Lab (early version, marked outdated)](https://www.blockchainresearchlab.org/2021/02/08/the-musk-effect-how-elon-musks-tweets-affect-the-cryptocurrency-market/)
- Undergraduate event study (Univ. of Kent, Apr 2019 - Jan 2022), low-weight evidence: Musk's DOGE "meme" tweets had -9.43% abnormal returns against +11.41% for non-meme tweets. — [Kent PDF](https://media.www.kent.ac.uk/se/29936/KheireddineAdeyemiCryptocurrancyimpactsofTweeting.pdf)
- **Industry (CoinWire, 2024, via Benzinga), not peer-reviewed.** Of 1,567 memecoins promoted by 377 X influencers, **86% lost 90%+ of their value within three months**. For influencers with more than 200k followers, 89% of promoted tokens had negative returns. — [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/cryptocurrency/24/11/42169183/dont-get-fooled-by-the-success-of-dogecoin-shiba-inu-76-of-x-twitter-influencers-promote-d)

### Inferences
- KOL mentions follow the same overreaction pattern as general attention (Section 1), only stronger and faster. Any positive window is likely minutes to hours, and paid promoters and their insiders probably capture it before the tweet.
- The Musk/DOGE effect is a special case: a single, very influential account and a liquid coin. It does not show that KOL tweets on illiquid microcaps can be traded profitably.

### Gaps
- I found no peer-reviewed study that separates disclosed paid promotions from organic KOL mentions and measures returns for each.
- A "2025 Marquette University study" on negative returns after influencer recommendations appeared only in blog form, and I could not locate it, so it is excluded.

## 4. Memecoin / pump.fun launch survival statistics (2024-2026)

### Takeaway
Most launchpad tokens die almost immediately. About 69% of pump.fun tokens stop trading on launch day, about 80% within a day, and about 4.6% last more than 90 days. Only about 0.2-1.7% "graduate" off the bonding curve, depending on period and measurement. Vendor rug/scam rates (about 98.7%) depend on the definition and should not be compared with lifespan figures.

### Cited Findings
- **CoinGecko, "The Average Lifespan of Pump.fun Memecoins Is Less Than a Day" (updated Jun 23, 2026; 18,675,645 traded tokens launched Jan 14, 2024 - Jun 18, 2026).** **68.67%** (12.8M) traded for the last time on launch day. About **80.37%** were gone within 1 day. About 12.1% lasted more than 7 days and about 7.5% more than 30 days (both derived from CoinGecko's table). **4.55%** (850,180) lasted more than 90 days. Caveat: only pump.fun bonding-curve trades are counted, so lifespans of graduated tokens trading on Raydium, Meteora or PumpSwap are understated. Graduation is about 1%. The study measures trading life, not fraud. — [CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/average-lifespan-of-pumpfun-tokens)
- **Bitquery (Oct 2026, via crypto.news), industry.** 79% of new tokens traded only on launch day (Solana tokens, Nov 1, 2025 - Oct 7, 2026). About 1 in 58 (about 1.7%) graduated. Across about 2.9M ordinary wallets in ten sampled launch weeks, **74% held less SOL than they spent within two weeks, after fees**. — [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/pump-fun-memecoin-trader-fatigue-token-survival/)
- **Marino, Naviglio, Tarantelli & Lillo, "Predicting the success of new crypto-tokens: the Pump.fun case" (arXiv 2602.14860, Feb 2026; Sep 1 - Oct 1, 2025).** 655,770 tokens from 243,123 creator addresses, with **4,338 graduated (about 0.63%)**. Of 184,282 tokens with at least 30 swaps, **92.22% had at least one detected dump**, and dumps before graduation are common because selling before graduation yields more SOL. — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.14860v1)
- **"Pump.fun Graduation Regime Windows: Survival Analysis of 832,941 Token Launches and the Social-Presence Effect" (arXiv 2607.02823 v3, corrected; May 8 - Jun 10, 2026).** Pooled graduation rate **0.198%** (95% CI 0.189-0.208%). Median time to graduation is 1.66 min, the 90th percentile 2.80 min, the maximum 5.98 min. Important caveat in the corrigendum: the collector saw only about 6 minutes after each launch, so all rates are lower bounds for the "fast regime". — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02823v3)
- **Mancino, "The Memecoin Phenomenon: An In-Depth Study of Solana's Blockchain Trends" (arXiv 2512.11850; Q4 2024).** Pump.fun made up to 71.1% of all Solana mints, peaking at 69,046 in a day. Under 2% graduated at peak. Pump.fun tokens were 40-67.4% of daily DEX transactions. Daily unique users peaked at about 260k (Nov 2024). The paper has no returns or rug statistics. — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.11850v3)
- **Solidus Labs (2025), vendor report.** About **98.7% of pump.fun tokens** and 93% of Raydium liquidity pools show characteristics of pump-and-dump or rug pull. The criteria are broad and not comparable to the lifespan studies. — [Solidus Labs](https://www.soliduslabs.com/reports/solana-rug-pulls-pump-dumps-crypto-compliance)
- **"Catching the Rug" (arXiv 2608.20271, 2026).** Defines a rug pull as a TVL drop of 99% or more, or idle time above 80% of lifetime. Under that definition, the background states that over 80% of memecoins rug. — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.20271v1)

### Inferences
- The base rate is the dominant fact. With graduation at about 0.2-1.7% and about 70-80% dead within a day, a social filter would need a very large lift just to break even, before costs.
- Graduation now happens within minutes (median 1.7 min in mid-2026; median about 4.4 min in the Sep 2025 sample per Marino et al.). Any social signal that has to be scraped, aggregated and acted on would have to fit inside that window.

### Gaps
- I found no rigorous study of the full return distribution for buyers of pump.fun tokens at different entry points (e.g., buying at graduation vs at launch), net of fees and MEV. Bitquery's 74% wallet-loss figure is the closest.
- Chainalysis's own figures on memecoin rug pulls and pump.fun were not retrieved in this pass.

## 5. Machine-learning papers using social features to detect early pumps or predict new-token success; precision and real-world tradability

### Takeaway
ML detects pumps and frauds well after the fact or within seconds (F1 about 0.95 on CEX pumps). Predicting new-token success before the fact is weak (MCC about 0.25-0.36 for rug prediction) and mostly uses on-chain features, not social ones. The one large launchpad study with social features finds a Telegram link raises graduation probability about 9-fold, but from a 0.17% base and without any trading claim. None of the papers model slippage, MEV or latency in a realistic way.

### Cited Findings
- **Social-presence effect on pump.fun (arXiv 2607.02823 v3, May-Jun 2026, 832,941 launches).** Graduation is **1.485% with a Telegram link vs 0.166% without (8.94x lift; Cox hazard ratio 5.40, 95% CI 4.73-6.17)**. Website: 0.264% vs 0.158% (HR 1.19). Twitter/X: 0.227% vs 0.149% (HR 1.31). All three channels together: 1.919% vs 0.110% with none (17.4x). Initial market cap is the strongest continuous predictor (HR 4.51 on log scale). Cox concordance is 0.858. The authors claim no causality and **make no trading or filter-deployment claims**. The Telegram lift is probably a lower bound because of the 6-minute coverage window. — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02823v3)
- **Marino et al. (arXiv 2602.14860, Sep 2025).** Uses only on-chain features: bot share, number of trades to reach a vSol level, presence of "successful traders", top creators. **No social features.** It reports no AUC or precision. Under buy-and-hold, a trade is profitable in expectation only if P(graduation) exceeds (vSol/115)². The baseline curve stays **below breakeven**, and most conditional curves do too. Only the top-10-creator conditioning exceeds breakeven, and that is based on few tokens. Fast liquidity build-up is the strongest predictor. Fees: 1.25% on the bonding curve, ignored in the breakeven analysis. MEV and latency are not studied. — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.14860v1)
- **"Catching the Rug: Early Prediction of Fraudulent Memecoins on Solana via ML" (arXiv 2608.20271, 2026).** XGBoost and Random Forest on early features across PumpFun and Raydium give in-domain **MCC of about 0.25-0.36** (modest), and the paper also tests cross-domain transfer. — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.20271v1)
- **La Morgia et al. (ACM TOIT 2023).** Detects Telegram/Discord pumps on CEXs **25 seconds after start, F1 94.5%**. This is detection after the start, not prediction. — [arXiv 2105.00733](https://export.arxiv.org/abs/2105.00733)
- **Xu & Livshits (USENIX Sec 2019).** Predicts the pump target before the announcement; the strategy returned up to 60% over about 2.5 months on small positions (Jun 2018 - Feb 2019). This is on CEXs with a small capital base, so it is not evidence of scalability. — [USENIX](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity19/presentation/xu-jiahua)

### Inferences
- Social-presence features (a Telegram/X/website link in the token metadata) are cheap, known at launch, and carry real lift. Even at 17x the base, though, about 98% of tokens with all three channels still fail to graduate, and graduation is not the same as profit for a buyer. Marino et al. show most conditioning signals stay below the buy-and-hold breakeven.
- The minute-scale graduation window, bot share often high, and the absence of MEV/sandwich modeling in all these papers mean reported predictiveness is an upper bound on what is tradable. Real execution would face sniper bots and sandwiching that the papers do not model.
- The most robust practical use of the evidence is defensive: social attention spikes and KOL mentions mark likely local tops and coins that will underperform over 10-90 days, not entries.

### Gaps
- I found no peer-reviewed paper that uses real-time X/Telegram mention velocity to predict memecoin returns (not graduation) with out-of-sample precision and backtests that include costs (slippage, Jito tips/MEV, latency).
- I found no independent replication of the social-presence lift outside the May-Jun 2026 window, which was itself limited to 6 minutes of observation.

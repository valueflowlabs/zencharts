# Stack more sats from Poland, cheaply

The main way a Polish software engineer accumulates more bitcoin is to raise the investable surplus and lose as little of it as possible on the way into cold storage. Buying tactics matter far less. Three levers decide the outcome. First, income structure (B2B vs UoP, ryczałt vs liniowy vs IP Box) can shift investable cash by thousands of PLN a month. Second, the on-ramp: converting PLN to EUR on a weekday in Revolut, then placing maker orders at a MiCA-licensed EU exchange, costs roughly **0.2–0.4% all-in**. Instant or "recurring buy" products cost **1–2.6%**. Third, tax hygiene: file PIT-38 every year you buy, so a cost pool builds up that shields later sales from the flat 19% rate. The regulatory backdrop is unusual. As of October 2026 Poland still has **no national MiCA act**. President Nawrocki vetoed the crypto-assets bill three times, and on 4 Sep 2026 the Sejm fell **25 votes short** of overriding the third veto. Polish users can therefore only be served lawfully by CASPs licensed elsewhere in the EU and passported into Poland. Zondacrypto (ex-BitBay) is bankrupt, and Binance is winding down for Polish clients. That makes self-custody on a well-designed, multi-vendor setup the default. Two 2026 events sharpened this: the Coldcard entropy flaw, which led to more than 1,000 BTC being drained, and a sharp rise in physical "wrench attacks" in Europe. Avoid yield, leverage and altcoins. Size the position so that a 75–80% drawdown never forces a sale. That one risk has destroyed more bitcoin stacks than any fee schedule.

## Your tax form moves more sats than any DCA trick

Senior Polish engineers earn a high fiat income, and they control how much of it survives tax and ZUS. Just Join IT postings put senior B2B rates around **24,360 PLN net of VAT per month**, with UoP at about 20,400 PLN gross ([Bankier](https://www.bankier.pl/wiadomosc/Koniec-Eldorado-Wynagrodzenia-w-IT-o-20-proc-nizsze-od-oczekiwan-specjalistow-8877072.html)). Architects reach about 30,240 PLN on B2B ([Just Join IT](https://justjoin.it/salary-report/category-statistics)), and other medians run 25,200–30,240 PLN ([itwiz.pl](https://itwiz.pl/10-najlepiej-platnych-zawodow-it-w-2025-roku/)). These are job-posting figures, and the UoP and B2B numbers are not directly comparable ([Just Join IT methodology](https://justjoin.it/salary-report/methodology)). For a JDG developer the choice is between **ryczałt 12% on revenue**, **liniowy 19%**, and **IP Box 5%** on qualified IP income, which is usually paired with liniowy ([freenance](https://freenance.io/przedsiebiorczosc/ip-box-5-procent-dla-programistow-2026-jak-skorzystac-step-by-step/); [Infor](https://ksiegowosc.infor.pl/podatki/ryczalt/5443784,Ryczalt-dla-programisty.html)). The health contribution sits on top. The President's August 2025 veto kept the Polski Ład structure in place. Liniowy pays **4.9% of income** with a minimum of 432.54 PLN a month. Ryczałt pays a flat **1,495.04 PLN a month above 300k PLN revenue** ([finwire.pl](https://finwire.pl/artykuly/skladka-zdrowotna-2026-minimum-432-zl-pis-powrot-sprzed-2022); [podatki.biz](https://www.podatki.biz/artykuly/zmiany-2026-skladka-na-ubezpieczenie-zdrowotne_34_60853.htm)). By rough arithmetic, the gap between the best and worst form for a solo developer on about 30k PLN a month is on the order of **2–4k PLN a month**. That is a bigger and more reliable sats lever than any market-timing rule. IP Box has a clock on it. The UD116 draft would require at least three unrelated employees, and it would pull IP Box income into the solidarity levy base. It missed 1 Jan 2026, and Infor considers 1 Jan 2027 more realistic ([Infor](https://ksiegowosc.infor.pl/podatki/ulgi/7493670,ip-box-2026-rzad-nie-zdazyl-zmiany-projekt-ud116-co-dalej-prace-trwaja-legislacja-ulgi-podatkowe-likwidacja-koszty-programowanie-programisci-outsourcing-koszty-pracy-podatki-mf-gov-pl.html)). Treat IP Box savings as a temporary windfall and consider putting them into bitcoin early, while the relief still exists.

On deployment, the evidence agrees with equity research: lump sum usually wins on raw returns, and DCA wins on drawdowns. A Bull Bitcoin study, reported second-hand, found lump sum beat DCA in **82.5% of 4.7 million simulated portfolios** from 2016 to 2025 ([Spark.money](https://www.spark.money/tools/bitcoin-dca-backtest-calculator)). Amdax and Galaxy both found DCA mainly cuts drawdowns and time underwater ([Amdax](https://www.amdax.com/en/research/guide/investing-in-bitcoin-which-strategy-works-best-2); [Galaxy](https://www.galaxy.app/insights/the-benefits-of-dollar-cost-averaging-into-bitcoin)). For someone paid monthly the debate is mostly moot, because buying on payday already is DCA. It matters only for a bonus or an existing cash pile, and there the odds favour deploying it within a few months. "Smart DCA" signals such as the Mayer Multiple's 2.4 "overheated" line or 200-week-MA weighting have **no independent backtest** behind them ([TheRightTrader](https://therighttrader.com/de/bitcoin-charts/mayer-multiple); [Payhip](https://payhip.com/b/0FwxA)). With only about four cycles of data, any rule tuned on them is likely overfitted. A fixed base DCA plus a discretionary "dip budget" is a reasonable compromise, but it is not a source of extra return.

Earning directly in BTC changes the mechanics more than the economics. Bitwage offers payroll conversion ([Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/4500-companies-now-pay-their-employees-bitcoin-using-bitwage)). Strike launched in Europe in 2024 for "eligible customers", but Poland is not named in the coverage ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/290637/bitcoin-lightning-app-strike-europe)). For a contractor, converting part of each invoice on a cheap exchange does the same job with less counterparty risk. The opportunity specific to engineers is open-source funding. OpenSats awarded **$2.33M in long-term-support grants plus $5.64M in other grants in 2025** ([OpenSats](https://opensats.org/blog/2025-year-in-review)), and it reports $21.6–27.6M allocated in total to 252–324 grantees ([OpenSats funds](https://opensats.org/funds); [Q2 2026 newsletter](https://opensats.org/newsletter/2026-Q2)). Grants usually pay less than senior Polish B2B rates. They suit engineers already contributing to Bitcoin Core, Lightning implementations, BDK or nostr. They are a mission choice, not a way to maximise sats per hour.

Home mining loses to buying. 2026 G11 tariffs run **1.04–1.32 PLN/kWh gross all-in** ([Rankomat](https://rankomat.pl/nieruchomosci/ceny-pradu); [Enerad](https://enerad.pl/ceny-pradu/)). At the late-September hashprice of $39.87/PH/s/day, the most efficient ASICs (under 14 J/TH) earn about **$138 per MWh** ([Hashrate Index](https://hashrateindex.com/blog/hashrate-index-roundup-september-28-2026/)). At roughly 3.6–3.8 PLN/USD (my assumption) that is about 0.50 PLN per kWh consumed, less than half the cost of the power. Mining only makes sense as a substitute for resistive electric heating in winter, or to absorb cheap midday PV surplus, and even then it is a hobby with one real benefit: sats with no KYC history. Card cashback is similarly marginal. Bybit EU offers 2–10% in BTC by tier ([PR Newswire](https://tools.prnewswire.com/en-us/live/20813/release/20260119EN65610)), and Satsback pays Lightning sats at more than 10,000 merchants and lists Poland ([Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/bitcoin-rewards-platform-satsback-v2-launches)). At a realistic 1%, 6,000 PLN a month of spending yields about 720 PLN a year. The top tiers require holding exchange tokens, which brings counterparty and altcoin risk.

## Weekday EUR plus maker orders costs a tenth of "instant buy"

The cheapest regulated route avoids PLN order books altogether. On the **Revolut Standard plan, PLN to EUR is free on weekdays up to 5,000 zł a month**. Above that it costs 1%, plus 1% on weekends. Plus raises the free limit to 15,000 zł, and Premium removes it ([moniaki](https://moniaki.pl/czy-revolut-jest-darmowy-oplaty-revolut/); [monito](https://www.monito.com/pl/wiki/limity-revolut)). Wise is quoted from about 0.33% ([Wise](https://wise.com/pl/blog/revolut-prowizja-za-przewalutowanie)). The converted EUR either goes by free SEPA to an order-book exchange or stays in Revolut for Revolut X. Fees on limit (maker) orders differ by an order of magnitude from fees on convenience buys:

Withdrawal fees are what keep Revolut X from being free. At 1,000 PLN a month, one quarterly withdrawal of about 3,000 PLN at roughly 10 PLN adds about 0.33%. The total therefore lands near **0.3%**, in the same range as Bitvavo plus a quarterly withdrawal. Kraken's fetched schedule shows a base tier higher than the 0.16–0.25% that Polish comparison sites still quote, which makes Kraken less competitive for small DCA. Check in-app before committing. Two PLN-native platforms with BLIK hold MiCA licences: **Bybit EU and OKX** ([cryps.pl](https://cryps.pl/ktore-gieldy-kryptowalut-maja-licencje-mica/)). OKX's BLIK pricing is reported as 0% up to 4,000 zł per transaction by one source and as quick-buy only at 0.20% by another ([bithub](https://bithub.pl/poradniki/kryptowaluty-blik/); [inwesthub](https://inwesthub.pl/rankingi/gieldy-kryptowalut)). Bybit's maker fee reportedly rose to 0.25% on 5 Oct 2026 ([ranking-gield-kryptowalut.pl](https://ranking-gield-kryptowalut.pl/)). Nobody has measured spreads on the thin PLN books, so the EUR route is the safer bet on cost. Monthly fixed fees also change with plan, so verify every figure in-app; the notes flag fee data as volatile and frequently contradictory across sources.

P2P platforms serve privacy rather than cost. RoboSats charges about 0.2% over Lightning, Peach charges buyers 2%, and Bisq now has an Android app ([spark.money](https://www.spark.money/tools/bitcoin-p2p-exchange-comparison)). Market premiums over spot were not quantified, and no Polish source rules on whether individual P2P trades are legal. The available commentary says MiCA enforcement targets platforms, not users ([Tangem](https://tangem.com/en/learning-hub/post/mica-poland-crypto-self-custody/)). PIT-38 obligations apply to P2P disposals all the same.

## Three vetoes left Poland without its own crypto regulator

Poland is described as the only EU state where a firm cannot obtain a domestic MiCA licence ([Tangem](https://tangem.com/en/learning-hub/post/mica-poland-crypto-self-custody/)). The crypto-assets act (*ustawa o rynku kryptoaktywów*) would have made KNF the supervisor. Nawrocki vetoed it in December 2025, in February 2026, and again on **11 June 2026**. He argued that over-regulation would push firms abroad, and the provisions in dispute were KNF powers to block accounts and websites ([PolsatNews](https://www.polsatnews.pl/wiadomosc/2026-09-04/ustawa-o-kryptowalutach-sejm-zdecydowal-ws-prezydenckiego-weta/); [GazetaPrawna](https://www.gazetaprawna.pl/wiadomosci/kraj/artykuly/11261394,weto-prezydenta-nawrockiego-ustawa-kryptowaluty-2026.html)). At the override vote on **4 Sep 2026**, 241 deputies voted for, against a 266 threshold ([rp.pl](https://www.rp.pl/prawo-w-polsce/art45096341-weto-prezydenta-ws-ustawy-o-kryptowalutach-sejm-nie-dal-rady-go-odrzucic); [PAP](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/weto-prezydenta-ws-kryptoaktywow-jest-decyzja-sejmu)). Competing drafts from Konfederacja, Polska 2050 and Petru/Ćwik exist ([prawo.pl](https://www.prawo.pl/biznes/kryptoaktywa-co-jest-w-projekcie-konfederacji,1539903.html); [polsatnews](https://www.polsatnews.pl/wiadomosc/2026-04-23/pn-nieoficjalnie-ustawa-polski-2050-o-kryptowalutach-jest-do-zaakceptowania-przez-prezydenta/)). I found no report of any act passing after 4 Sep, but check sejm.gov.pl to confirm. The MiCA transition ended on 1 July 2026. Since then a Polish VASP registry entry no longer authorises anything, and the only lawful route is an EU CASP licence plus a cross-border notification into Poland ([Woźniak Legal](https://wozniaklegal.com/en/news-and-insight/500/polands-mica-deadline-looms-no-casp-law-no-licenses-after-1-july-2026.html); [comparic.pl](https://comparic.pl/znamy-stanowisko-knf-ws-rynku-krypto-kluczowa-data-to-1-lipca-2026-r/)).

For a user, this means your effective protection comes from the exchange's home regulator, not from KNF. In practice that means the Central Bank of Ireland for Kraken, the CSSF for Coinbase and Bitstamp, the AFM for Bitvavo, the FMA for Bitpanda, CySEC for Revolut, the AMF for Relai, and the Bank of Latvia for Kanga EU ([Kraken](https://www.kraken.com/features/fee-schedule); [Finextra](https://finextra.com/pressarticle/106051/coinbase-secures-mica-license-in-luxembourg); [DefiLlama](https://defillama.com/mica/luxembourg); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/360216/bitvavo-largest-eur-spot-exchange-secures-mica-license-from-the-netherlands); [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/poland-kanga-secures-mica-license-latvia)). The domestic incumbents fared badly. **Zondacrypto's operator was declared bankrupt in Tallinn on 27 Aug 2026**, with at least **€431.9m of liabilities against about €167k of disclosed assets** ([TVN24](https://tvn24.pl/biznes/ze-swiata/bb-trade-estonia-ou-estonski-sad-oglosil-upadlosc-operatora-zondacrypto-st9209243); [money.pl](https://www.money.pl/gospodarka/432-mln-euro-dlugu-ujawniamy-co-estonski-sad-znalazl-w-zondacrypto-7325485051406432a.html)). The deadline for filing claims with the Estonian trustee is **today, 27 Oct 2026** ([TVN24](https://tvn24.pl/biznes/z-kraju/zondacrypto-poszkodowani-moga-zglaszac-wierzytelnosci-do-27-pazdziernika-st9300847)). Binance, with no MiCA licence, has allowed only withdrawals and reduce-only orders for Polish users since 1 July ([bitcoin.pl](https://bitcoin.pl/binance-przestanie-dzialac-w-polsce-od-1-lipca)). Kanga's Latvian licence does not cover the old Polish platform where most users hold accounts, and PLN spot on the new EU platform is promised only for "later stages" ([Forklog](https://forklog.com/en/polish-crypto-exchange-kanga-secures-mica-license-in-latvia/)). Zondacrypto's hot wallet reportedly fell from about 55.7 BTC to 0.18 BTC before withdrawals froze ([money.pl](https://www.money.pl/gospodarka/wielki-problem-zondacrypto-klienci-sie-skarza-mamy-niepokojaca-analize-7272365494077632a.html)). That is the strongest local argument for treating any exchange as a short-term transit point only.

## Filing PIT-38 every year turns purchases into a tax shield

Polish crypto tax is simple and, for a long-term holder, generous. Disposals of virtual currency for fiat, goods, services or non-crypto property rights are taxed at a **flat 19% in a separate PIT-38 basket** under art. 30b ust. 1a. **Crypto-to-crypto swaps, including stablecoins, and transfers between your own wallets are tax-neutral** ([ifirma.pl](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/jak-rozliczac-sie-z-kryptowalut-w-2026-roku/); [pitax.pl](https://www.pitax.pl/wiedza/poradnik-rozliczenia/jak-rozliczyc-kryptowaluty-w-pit-38/)). The regime pools costs instead of matching lots. Documented purchase costs and exchange fees are deductible, and any excess of costs over revenue **rolls forward indefinitely**. This is not a "loss" subject to the 5-year/50% rule. The catch is that you must **file PIT-38 for every year in which you bought, even with no sale** ([ifirma.pl](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/jak-rozliczac-sie-z-kryptowalut-w-2026-roku/)). An accumulator who files diligently builds a cost pool that covers future spending or partial sales up to that amount tax-free. One who skips years has to reconstruct the history later, and from 2026 the reconstruction faces scrutiny. The basket is also isolated: crypto cost surpluses cannot offset stock or ETF gains. Paying a merchant in BTC, including over Lightning, is a disposal at the market value of what you receive ([ifirma.pl](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/jak-rozliczac-sie-z-kryptowalut-w-2026-roku/)). The Ministry of Finance confirmed in the Zondacrypto context that revenue arises at the moment of exchange, even if the platform never pays out. Stolen or lost crypto is not a disposal, and its purchase cost remains deductible.

How you earn BTC sets your cost basis. A B2B invoice settled in BTC is business revenue at its PLN value, subject to the JDG's income tax and health contribution, and that value becomes the BTC's acquisition cost for later PIT-38 purposes ([akademialtca.pl](https://akademialtca.pl/blog/zaplata-za-usluge-w-kryptowalucie-a-rozliczenie-podatkowe)). It is not a tax dodge, but the BTC is not taxed twice. Mining, staking and airdrops are contested. KIS has sometimes taxed them on receipt, but the administrative courts and the NSA hold that tax arises only on disposal, with zero cost basis. Electricity and hardware are not deductible acquisition costs ([NSA](https://orzeczenia.nsa.gov.pl/doc/889EBCD450.rtf); [divly](https://divly.com/pl/przewodniki/staking-i-airdropy-podatek); [podatki.biz](https://www.podatki.biz/artykuly/wykopywanie-kryptowalut-i-podatki_65_51888.htm)). Private PIT-38 gains do not enter a JDG's health contribution base ([akademialtca.pl](https://akademialtca.pl/blog/dzialalnosc-w-zakresie-obrotu-kryptowalutami-a-skladka-zdrowotna)), although no ZUS document confirming this was retrieved.

For a large exit, there are two thresholds to plan around. The **4% solidarity levy** applies to total income above PLN 1M, and the statutory wording of art. 30h includes art. 30b without carving out the crypto paragraph ([arslege](https://arslege.pl/ustawa-o-podatku-dochodowym-od-osob-fizycznych/k71/s13007/)). Spreading sales across tax years so each year's total stays under PLN 1M legally avoids the extra four points. **Exit tax** (art. 30da) applies only when transferred assets exceed PLN 4M, and whether private crypto falls under it is disputed in the commentary ([podatki.biz](https://www.podatki.biz/artykuly/exit-tax-od-4-mln-zlotych_64_41147.htm); [kancelaria-skarbiec](https://kancelaria-skarbiec.pl/?p=28194)). Gifts to group 0 relatives are exempt from gift tax if notified on SD-Z2 within 6 months. The recipient, however, gets no deductible purchase cost, so the full 19% is deferred rather than avoided ([ifirma.pl](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/jak-rozliczac-sie-z-kryptowalut-w-2026-roku/)).

Enforcement visibility is rising. Poland's **DAC8/CARF act** was adopted on 13 Feb 2026. CASPs collect data from 1 Jan 2026 and report 2026 activity to KAS by **30 June 2027** ([Vialto](https://vialtopartners.com/regional-alerts/poland-global-mobility-tax-new-reporting-obligations-for-crypto-asset-income-what-is-changing-for-individuals-2); [infor.pl](https://www.infor.pl/twoje-pieniadze/podatki/7538305,koniec-z-ukrywaniem-kryptowalut-przed-skarbowka-prezydent-podpisal-ustawe-ktora-implementuje-dyrektywy-unijne-dac8-i-dac9-wymiana-informacji-podatkowych-miedzy-panstwami-raje-podatkowe.html)). Anyone with undeclared earlier disposals has a narrow window to correct them before cross-checks begin. Finally, there is a wrapper alternative. BTC ETNs such as CoinShares BITC and VanEck VBTC are available at XTB ([financemagnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/cant-buy-bitcoin-etfs-in-europe-xtb-has-a-solution-with-new-crypto-etns/amp); [xtb.com](https://www.xtb.com/int/etns/bitc-de)). They are taxed as securities, so their gains and losses net against stocks. If a broker allows them inside an IKE (2026 limit **PLN 28,260**), that would be the only fully tax-free route to BTC price exposure ([strefainwestorow](https://strefainwestorow.pl/emerytura/limit-ike-ikze-2026)). I could not confirm that any Polish broker permits this, so ask your broker directly. ETNs give price exposure, not bitcoin you hold yourself.

## Most stacks are lost to bad custody, forced selling or yield

Self-custody is necessary, and 2026 showed it is not simple. On **30 Jul 2026 Coinkite disclosed that a 2021 firmware change** had made Coldcards generate seeds with a weak software RNG. The flaw left about **40 bits of entropy on Mk2/Mk3 and about 72 bits on Mk4/Mk5/Q**, against the expected 128. Galaxy counted **1,196 addresses drained for about 1,082.65 BTC** on the first day ([Forklog](https://forklog.com/en/kraken-cso-says-coldcard-flaw-exposes-gap-in-hardware-wallet-testing/); [Forklog](https://forklog.com/en/?p=101360)). Firmware updates do not repair existing seeds. Affected users must generate a new seed on fixed firmware and sweep their funds. A strong BIP-39 passphrase or at least 50 dice rolls would have mitigated the flaw ([Casa](https://blog.casa.io/coldcard-vulnerability/)). Kraken's CSO pointed out the general lesson: audits confirm that a good RNG exists, not that production firmware actually uses it. The robust design for a technical user is therefore a **2-of-3 multisig across vendors** (for example Trezor, BitBox02 and Jade), coordinated in Sparrow or Electrum, with the output descriptor backed up alongside every seed ([onrampbitcoin](https://onrampbitcoin.com/knowledge-center/hardware-wallet-comparison-2026); [BitBox](https://bitbox.swiss/bitbox02/compare-hardware-wallets/)). The minimum acceptable alternative is single-sig with user-added entropy and a passphrase. Stamp the seeds on steel, store them in separate locations, test recovery with a small amount first, and leave a non-technical inheritance letter that keeps the seed and passphrase in different places. Withdraw rarely and in large amounts. Many small DCA withdrawals create UTXOs that are expensive to consolidate later ([Spark.money](https://www.spark.money/tools/bitcoin-dca-backtest-calculator)). Fees were only 0.70% of block rewards in late September ([Hashrate Index](https://hashrateindex.com/blog/hashrate-index-roundup-september-28-2026/)), so this is a cheap period for consolidation.

Physical risk is now real in Europe. CertiK counted **72 wrench attacks worldwide in 2025 (+75%)**. Europe's share rose to over 40%, led by France, and rose again in early 2026 ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/356604/41m-in-losses-as-crypto-wrench-attacks-hit-record-high-in-2025); [CertiK](https://www-cn.certik.com/blog/2026-wrench-attacks-overview)). In Poland, the only case found is a foiled kidnapping plot against a Małopolska crypto trader that predates 2025 ([Wprost](https://www.wprost.pl/10438206)). Countermeasures cost little. Never disclose holdings. Keep one multisig key geographically distant so funds cannot be moved under duress at home, and keep only a small decoy hot wallet. Note that CEIDG publishes JDG addresses, so a virtual-office address is worth considering.

Market risks are the other way stacks are lost. Bitcoin has had four drawdowns of roughly **75–93%** (2011, 2014–15, 2018, 2022), and bottoms came 12–13 months after declines began ([K33](https://k33.com/research/archive/articles/the-2022-bear-market-is-on-track-with-the-2018-bear-market); [NYDIG](https://nydig.com/research/charting-drawdowns-during-up-cycles)). At 2x leverage, a 50% fall liquidates you, and every cycle has exceeded that. Yield fails in a quieter way. FTX's "100% recovery" was paid at **November 2022 USD prices** ([The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/cefi/ftx-s-bankruptcy-plan-approved-clearing-path-for-98-customer-repayments)). BlockFi's unsecured creditors were estimated at 35–63% ([Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/ip-law/blockfi-cleared-to-repay-customers-through-liquidation-plan)). Measured in bitcoin, anyone who chased a 1–5% yield lost most of their stack, and Zondacrypto has just repeated that lesson locally. The protection is a cash buffer: about 6–12 months of expenses plus tax and ZUS reserves for a JDG worker, who has no sick pay. Add to that a position size you could hold through a 75–80% fall without changing how you live.

## Conclusion

Poland's regulatory gap has a side effect. With no domestic licensing path, accumulation runs on EU-passported exchanges and self-custody, which is the setup a sats-maximiser should want anyway. The Zondacrypto collapse shows the cost of trusting a local incumbent without a strong regulator behind it. The best-value system is mundane: optimise the JDG form while IP Box lasts, convert PLN on weekdays, place maker orders, withdraw quarterly to a multi-vendor multisig, and file PIT-38 every year. Its edge comes from avoiding 1–2% fees and catastrophic losses, not from predicting price.

Three dates are worth acting on. Today (27 Oct 2026) is the Zondacrypto claims deadline for anyone affected. The first DAC8 reports reach KAS by 30 June 2027, which closes the window for quietly correcting old filings. And 1 Jan 2027 is the likely date the IP Box restriction takes effect. Several facts in this report remain unverified: a possible fourth Polish crypto-assets act, current exchange fee tiers, PLN order-book spreads, and whether BTC ETNs are allowed in an IKE. Check these directly before acting, because each one could change the ranking.

## Polish tax treatment of bitcoin/crypto for a Polish-resident individual (as of Oct 2026)

Scope: individual Polish tax resident, private (non-business) holdings unless noted. Not legal advice. Statutory references are to ustawa o PIT (u.p.d.o.f.) unless stated. Research date: 2026-10-27. Several primary sources (isap.sejm.gov.pl, eureka.mf.gov.pl) were not fetched directly in this pass; statutory article content below comes from secondary sources quoting the Act and is flagged where relevant.

### 1. PIT-38 core rules for virtual currencies (rate, swaps, cost carry-forward, loss segregation, filing without a sale)

#### Takeaway
Gains from "odpłatne zbycie waluty wirtualnej" are taxed at a flat 19% in a separate basket on PIT-38 (section E). Crypto-to-crypto swaps are tax-neutral. Purchase costs are deductible only within the crypto basket. Unused costs ("nadwyżka kosztów") roll forward indefinitely. You must file PIT-38 for any year in which you incurred purchase costs, even with no sale. Crypto results cannot be netted against stock/ETF gains.

#### Cited Findings
- Rate and basket: crypto income is taxed at 19% on PIT-38 section E under art. 30b ust. 1a. It is a separate source of income, not combined with business income or other capital income (stocks, under art. 30b ust. 1), and no tax-free allowance or reliefs apply — [ifirma.pl, 2026](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/jak-rozliczac-sie-z-kryptowalut-w-2026-roku/). The prohibition on combining crypto income with art. 30b ust. 1 / art. 27 / art. 30c income is also quoted in [podatki.biz search snippet / danina context](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/danina-solidarnosciowa-co-stanowi-podstawe-opodatkowania.html).
- Taxable event (art. 17 ust. 1f): disposal means exchanging virtual currency for legal tender, for goods, services or property rights other than virtual currency, or using it to pay obligations — [ifirma.pl](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/jak-rozliczac-sie-z-kryptowalut-w-2026-roku/).
- Tax-neutral: crypto-to-crypto swaps (including stablecoins), transfers between your own wallets or exchanges, PoS and PoW/mining generate no PIT income and are not reported — [ifirma.pl](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/jak-rozliczac-sie-z-kryptowalut-w-2026-roku/). Several other guides agree, while one freenance.io guide claims swaps are taxable. That claim conflicts with the statute and the majority view — [pitax.pl](https://www.pitax.pl/wiedza/poradnik-rozliczenia/jak-rozliczyc-kryptowaluty-w-pit-38/); contradicted by [freenance.io](https://freenance.io/podatki/podatek-od-krypto-binance-coinbase-kraken-2026-pit-38-zagraniczne-gielda-rozliczenie/).
- Exception: exchanging crypto for an NFT is taxable, because tax authorities treat NFTs as property rights, not virtual currency — [ifirma.pl](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/jak-rozliczac-sie-z-kryptowalut-w-2026-roku/).
- Costs (art. 22 ust. 14): deductible costs are documented expenses directly tied to acquiring the crypto, plus disposal costs including intermediary fees. Financing costs (loans) are not deductible. Neither are mining hardware or electricity. Acceptable proof includes exchange histories, bank transfer confirmations, kantor receipts and invoices — [ifirma.pl](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/jak-rozliczac-sie-z-kryptowalut-w-2026-roku/).
- Carry-forward (art. 22 ust. 16): the excess of costs over crypto revenue in a year increases crypto costs of the following year — [pitax.pl](https://www.pitax.pl/wiedza/poradnik-rozliczenia/jak-rozliczyc-kryptowaluty-w-pit-38/). This is not a "loss" with the 5-year/50% rule. It rolls over indefinitely — [ifirma.pl](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/jak-rozliczac-sie-z-kryptowalut-w-2026-roku/); also stated by podatnik.info ("rolowany bezterminowo"), per search snippet of [podatnik.info report](https://www.podatnik.info/raporty/krypto-iluzja-podatnik.info.pdf.pagespeed.ce.O4PiwYrcwk.pdf). Some freenance.io pages wrongly describe 5-year loss deduction — [freenance.io](https://freenance.io/kryptowaluty/krypto-pit-38-2026-jak-rozliczyc-zyski-straty-koszty-odliczenia-kalkulator).
- Filing without a sale: costs incurred in a year must be reported on PIT-38 even if nothing was sold, so they can reduce future taxable income — [ifirma.pl](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/jak-rozliczac-sie-z-kryptowalut-w-2026-roku/).
- Loss segregation: crypto cost surpluses offset only future crypto income, not other capital income such as stocks — [ifirma.pl](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/jak-rozliczac-sie-z-kryptowalut-w-2026-roku/).
- Deadline: 30 April of the following year (the 2025 return was due 30 April 2026) — [ifirma.pl](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/jak-rozliczac-sie-z-kryptowalut-w-2026-roku/); [pitax.pl](https://www.pitax.pl/wiedza/poradnik-rozliczenia/jak-rozliczyc-kryptowaluty-w-pit-38/).
- Revenue timing: the Ministry of Finance said, in the context of the Zondacrypto withdrawal problems (2026), that revenue arises when crypto is exchanged for fiat, even if the exchange has not paid out the funds — [ifirma.pl](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/jak-rozliczac-sie-z-kryptowalut-w-2026-roku/); see also [pitax.pl on delayed withdrawals](https://www.pitax.pl/wiedza/aktualnosci/opoznienie-wyplaty/).
- Stolen or lost crypto is not a taxable event, but the documented purchase cost remains deductible. Interpretations cited: 0115-KDIT1.4011.111.2024.2.MN (22 Apr 2024) and 0113-KDIPT2-3.4011.859.2025.2.SJ (22 Jan 2026) — [ifirma.pl](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/jak-rozliczac-sie-z-kryptowalut-w-2026-roku/).
- History: the current regime (separate section, 19%, cost pooling) has applied since 2019 — [ifirma.pl](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/jak-rozliczac-sie-z-kryptowalut-w-2026-roku/).

#### Inferences
- The regime uses cost pooling, not lot matching: total costs incurred minus total revenue, per year, with any surplus carried forward. FIFO and similar methods are not needed for crypto (unlike securities). For a long-term accumulator who never sells, every year's purchases build a growing carried-forward cost pool. This only works if PIT-38 is filed every year in which purchases occur.
- Because swaps are neutral, rebalancing between BTC and stablecoins on-chain or on exchanges does not trigger tax. Only off-ramping to fiat, goods, services or NFTs does.
- Partial sales can be sized so that revenue does not exceed the accumulated cost pool, giving zero tax in that year while the pool is used up.

#### Gaps
- I did not fetch the consolidated statutory text from isap.sejm.gov.pl. Paragraph numbering (art. 22 ust. 15 vs ust. 16) comes from secondary sources and memory, so verify it before citing in a formal document.
- I found no authoritative position on whether fiat-to-stablecoin purchases (e.g. PLN to USDC) count as a "purchase cost" event. The general rule suggests yes (buying virtual currency for fiat), and stablecoin to fiat is a disposal.

### 2. Solidarity levy (danina solidarnościowa, 4% above PLN 1M)

#### Takeaway
On the statutory wording, crypto gains fall within the levy base. Art. 30h ust. 2 includes income taxed under "art. 30b" without excluding ust. 1a, and art. 30b ust. 1a is the crypto provision. So combined income above PLN 1M from salary, business (linear or scale) and capital gains, including crypto, triggers a 4% levy on the excess. One MF explanation cites only specific sub-paragraphs of art. 30b, which creates some ambiguity.

#### Cited Findings
- Art. 30h ust. 1: a 4% levy on the base. Ust. 2: the base is the excess over PLN 1,000,000 of the sum of income taxed under art. 27 ust. 1, 9 and 9a, art. 30b, art. 30c and art. 30f, less social insurance contributions — [arslege.pl, art. 30h](https://arslege.pl/ustawa-o-podatku-dochodowym-od-osob-fizycznych/k71/s13007/).
- An NSA ruling confirms the base covers income under "art. 27 ust. 1, 9 i 9a, art. 30b, art. 30c" — [orzeczenia.nsa.gov.pl](https://orzeczenia.nsa.gov.pl/doc/86B3843927.rtf).
- ifirma states that if income exceeds PLN 1M, the levy applies in addition to PIT on crypto — [ifirma.pl](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/jak-rozliczac-sie-z-kryptowalut-w-2026-roku/). sum.money (a commercial, lower-reliability source) also says the 4% levy applies to crypto and capital income above PLN 1M — [sum.money](https://sum.money/pl/kryptowaluty-podatek/).
- Ambiguity: the MF explanations (objaśnienia) on the levy, as summarized by podatki.biz, refer to specific sub-paragraphs of art. 30b (ust. 5a, 5b, 5e, 5f) — [podatki.biz](https://www.podatki.biz/artykuly/mf-o-daninie-solidarnosciowej-objasnienia-podatkowe_34_42057.htm). Those paragraphs concern foreign tax credits/settlement, which suggests the reference is not a carve-out of ust. 1a, but this was not verified.
- The levy is declared on DSF-1 (general knowledge; not sourced in this pass).

#### Inferences
- A one-year exit of a large BTC position (more than ~PLN 1M of gain) can raise the marginal rate from 19% to 23% on the excess. Spreading disposals across tax years so that each year's total income stays at or below PLN 1M avoids the levy legally. This is a meaningful lever for a long-term holder.

#### Gaps
- I found no individual interpretation (eureka.mf.gov.pl) explicitly confirming crypto inclusion. Recommend searching eureka for "danina solidarnościowa waluty wirtualne".

### 3. Earning BTC: salary/B2B paid in BTC, mining, staking/lending, airdrops, cashback, Lightning

#### Takeaway
BTC received as remuneration is income from that source (employment or business), valued in PLN at receipt. Per KIS, that PLN value becomes the BTC's acquisition cost for later PIT-38 purposes, so an immediate sale gives roughly zero crypto income, but it must still be reported. Mining, staking and airdrops: KIS has sometimes taxed them on receipt, but administrative courts and the NSA hold that, under art. 17 ust. 1f, tax arises only on disposal (to fiat, goods or services), with zero cost basis. Cashback and Lightning are not specifically addressed in any source found.

#### Cited Findings
- B2B/business paid in crypto: a KIS interpretation treats receipt as acquisition. The value at receipt is the acquisition cost on later disposal, so an immediate sale at the same rate can mean zero income, but revenue and costs must still be reported on PIT-38 — [akademialtca.pl](https://akademialtca.pl/blog/zaplata-za-usluge-w-kryptowalucie-a-rozliczenie-podatkowe).
- ifirma's worked example: a business records revenue at the invoice value in the invoice year. A later sale of the crypto for PLN is capital income on PIT-38 for the year of sale, with the invoice value as the cost. The invoice may name crypto as the payment method, but VAT must be stated in PLN (art. 106e ust. 1 VAT Act) — [ifirma.pl](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/jak-rozliczac-sie-z-kryptowalut-w-2026-roku/).
- PLN valuation of crypto income uses market prices, with the average rate from the day before the income date per one guide — [akademialtca.pl](https://akademialtca.pl/blog/zaplata-za-usluge-w-kryptowalucie-a-rozliczenie-podatkowe). For employee salary in crypto, see [prawo.pl, "Jak opodatkować wynagrodzenie w kryptowalutach"](https://www.prawo.pl/biznes/jak-opodatkowac-wynagrodzenie-w-kryptowalutach,525047.html) (not fetched in full).
- Mining: the NSA has held that electricity and hardware are not direct acquisition costs under art. 22 ust. 14 — [podatki.biz](https://www.podatki.biz/artykuly/wykopywanie-kryptowalut-i-podatki_65_51888.htm); [ifirma.pl](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/jak-rozliczac-sie-z-kryptowalut-w-2026-roku/). Mining run as a business (JDG) is taxed as business income instead — [freenance.io](https://freenance.io/kryptowaluty/mining-kryptowalut-pl-2026-podatek-dochodowy-koszty-elektryka-procedura-jdg-spolka-przyklad).
- Staking and airdrops: there are conflicting positions. Some guides cite 2024-2025 KIS interpretations taxing on receipt. Voivodeship administrative courts (WSA) consistently overturn these, citing art. 17 ust. 1f. An NSA ruling says income arises only on exchange for legal tender or goods and services, and treats staking and airdrops like mining. Practitioners recommend an individual interpretation for active staking — [divly.com](https://divly.com/pl/przewodniki/staking-i-airdropy-podatek); [freenance.io](https://freenance.io/kryptowaluty/staking-airdrop-defi-podatek-2026-jak-rozliczyc-krypto-moment-uzyskania-przychodu); [NSA ruling](https://orzeczenia.nsa.gov.pl/doc/889EBCD450.rtf).
- DAC8 reports will include staking income — [ifirma.pl](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/jak-rozliczac-sie-z-kryptowalut-w-2026-roku/).

#### Inferences
- Receiving salary or invoices in BTC is not a tax-avoidance tool. The full PLN value is taxed as employment or business income (with ZUS and health contributions). But it does create a cost basis in the crypto basket equal to that value, so the BTC is not taxed again except on later appreciation.
- Lending yield paid in BTC (CeFi lending) was not specifically covered. By analogy with the court view on staking, it is taxed on disposal, but KIS may argue it is "other income" or capital income. Treat it as uncertain.
- Cashback in BTC from a card: no source found. Possible characterizations are a rebate on the purchase (not income) or a disposal-free acquisition at zero cost (taxed on sale). Uncertain.
- Lightning payments: Lightning is BTC, so paying for goods over Lightning is a disposal (see section 4). Opening or closing channels and moving funds between your own wallets is neutral, as a transfer between own wallets.

#### Gaps
- No source found for BTC card cashback, Lightning, or CeFi lending interest specifically.
- The exact KIS/NSA signatures for staking were not extracted. The NSA document link was found via search but not read in full.

### 4. Paying for goods/services with BTC

#### Cited Findings
- Disposal includes exchange for goods, services, property rights and payment of obligations in crypto — [ifirma.pl](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/jak-rozliczac-sie-z-kryptowalut-w-2026-roku/).

#### Inferences
- With a large carried-forward cost pool, small everyday BTC spending usually produces no tax due, but it still has to be reported as revenue.

### 6. Emigration/exit tax; inheritance and gifts of crypto

#### Takeaway
Exit tax (art. 30da) applies to unrealized gains on a change of residence only if the total market value of transferred assets exceeds PLN 4M. Whether private crypto is covered is disputed in the commentary. Gifts and inheritances of crypto fall under the inheritance and gift tax act. Group 0 (spouse, descendants, ascendants, siblings, step-parents) is exempt if notified on SD-Z2 within 6 months. A recipient who later sells may have no deductible purchase cost.

#### Cited Findings
- Art. 30da: income is the market value on the day before the residence change minus tax value. It does not apply if total transferred assets are at or below PLN 4,000,000 — [podatki.biz](https://www.podatki.biz/artykuly/exit-tax-od-4-mln-zlotych_64_41147.htm); [pitax.pl](https://www.pitax.pl/wiedza/sytuacje-podatkowe/exit-tax-czyli-podatek-od-wyjscia-zasady-funkcjonowania-w-pit/).
- Disputed crypto scope: one commentary claims crypto is outside exit tax, so moving residence avoids Polish tax. Another says exit tax covers all assets where crypto is held in a business — [kancelaria-skarbiec.pl](https://kancelaria-skarbiec.pl/?p=28194); [rulecalc.com](https://rulecalc.com/pl/podatki/upit-art-30da-niezrealizowane-zyski) (both low-to-medium reliability, no statutory citation).
- Gifts: gift tax applies (art. 9 and 15 of the inheritance and gift tax act). The recipient files SD-3 within 1 month. Group 0 is exempt with SD-Z2 within 6 months. Tax-free thresholds: PLN 36,120 (group I), 27,090 (II), 5,733 (III), counted over 5 years — [ifirma.pl](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/jak-rozliczac-sie-z-kryptowalut-w-2026-roku/).
- A recipient selling gifted crypto pays 19% PIT with no purchase-cost deduction. The 6-month rule that exempts other gifted assets (art. 10 ust. 1 pkt 8) does not apply to virtual currency — [ifirma.pl](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/jak-rozliczac-sie-z-kryptowalut-w-2026-roku/).

#### Inferences
- For inter-generational transfer, gifting or inheriting BTC within group 0 avoids gift tax, but the recipient takes a zero (or low) cost basis. The deferred 19% PIT on the full value remains latent.
- For accumulators planning emigration, holdings below PLN 4M are outside exit tax regardless. Above that, the art. 30da coverage of virtual currency should be checked against the statute or an interpretation.

#### Gaps
- Not verified from the statute or KIS whether waluta wirtualna is a "składnik majątku" for art. 30da on private holdings, or whether an heir inherits the decedent's cost basis (no source found).

### 7. ZUS / health contribution (składka zdrowotna) for a JDG owner

#### Takeaway
Private crypto gains taxed on PIT-38 (art. 30b ust. 1a) are capital income, not business income. They do not enter the health contribution base of a JDG on scale, linear or ryczałt, and do not create ZUS social contributions. The exception is crypto activity run within the business (e.g. systematic mining or trading recorded in the JDG), which is business income.

#### Cited Findings
- The JDG health contribution base is business income; crypto disposal income is capital income on PIT-38 — [akademialtca.pl](https://akademialtca.pl/blog/dzialalnosc-w-zakresie-obrotu-kryptowalutami-a-skladka-zdrowotna); [podatki.biz](https://www.podatki.biz/artykuly/kryptowaluty-w-rozliczeniu-rocznym_61_49687.htm).
- Crypto received as payment for an invoice is business revenue (subject to the JDG's tax and health rules) at the invoice value. The later sale is PIT-38 — [ifirma.pl](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/jak-rozliczac-sie-z-kryptowalut-w-2026-roku/).

#### Inferences
- PIT-38 capital income is generally outside the health contribution base after Polski Ład (2022). No source found says crypto is an exception. Confidence: medium-high, but no ZUS/KIS document was retrieved.

#### Gaps
- No official ZUS or KIS statement retrieved explicitly confirming the exclusion.

### 8. BTC ETPs/ETNs at Polish brokers; IKE/IKZE

#### Takeaway
BTC ETNs/ETPs (e.g. CoinShares BTCetc/BITC, VanEck VBTC) are listed securities. Gains on them are taxed under art. 30b ust. 1 (securities), reported via PIT-8C from a Polish broker, and can be netted against stock and ETF gains and losses (5-year loss carry-forward). That is unlike direct BTC. XTB offers BTC ETNs on its standard account. ### 1. Polish regulatory status: MiCA, the crypto-assets act, KNF, VASP register

#### Takeaway
Poland still has no national MiCA implementing act. President Nawrocki vetoed the crypto-assets act (ustawa o rynku kryptoaktywów, with KNF as supervisor) three times: Dec 2025, Feb 2026, 11 Jun 2026. The Sejm failed to override the third veto on 4 Sep 2026 (241 for, 266 needed). The MiCA transitional period ended 1 Jul 2026. The Polish VASP register no longer authorises crypto services. Only CASPs licensed in another EU state and passported into Poland may lawfully serve Polish clients.

### 5. Worked example: total cost per 1,000 PLN (DCA)

## Bitcoin Accumulation Strategies and Self-Custody for a Polish Software Engineer (2026)

Research date: 2026-10-27. Market context at last data point: BTC spot about $83,479 on 2026-09-28, YTD -4.5% ([Hashrate Index, 28 Sep 2026](https://hashrateindex.com/blog/hashrate-index-roundup-september-28-2026/)).

General caveat on sources: most of the quantitative "backtest" material for bitcoin comes from exchanges, brokers or affiliate sites, not from peer-reviewed research. It is marked as such below.

### DCA vs lump sum, and "smart DCA" variants (value averaging, Mayer Multiple, 200-week MA)

#### Takeaway
The evidence is mostly vendor-produced, and it says the same thing as equity research. Lump sum beats DCA on raw returns most of the time, because BTC has trended up. DCA gives lower drawdowns and similar or better risk-adjusted returns. For an employee or contractor, DCA mostly just means "buy when you get paid". I found no independent, transparent backtest showing that Mayer Multiple or 200-week-MA timing beats plain DCA.

#### Cited Findings
- Bull Bitcoin study (as reported second-hand): about 4.7 million simulated portfolios, Jan 2016 to May 2025. "Lump sum investing outperforms DCA in 82.5% of cases, especially over longer timeframes." Risk-adjusted returns were reportedly near-identical. I could not locate the original report, so this is unverified — [Nostr repost via damus.io](https://damus.io/nevent1qqszkw58jmkyma25677vy3tdncrv236j2ayja95fxt6r539lcdmm0hccqw3ww); [summary via WebSearch of Spark.money](https://www.spark.money/tools/bitcoin-dca-backtest-calculator)
- Spark.money attributes a reward-to-risk ratio to the same Bull Bitcoin study: 0.76 for DCA vs 0.53 for lump sum. This is a single secondary source, and it slightly contradicts the "near-identical" framing — [Spark.money](https://www.spark.money/tools/bitcoin-dca-backtest-calculator)
- Bull Bitcoin reportedly warns that very small on-chain DCA purchases (e.g. $10/week) create many small UTXOs that are costly to consolidate later — [Spark.money summary](https://www.spark.money/tools/bitcoin-dca-backtest-calculator)
- Amdax (Dutch asset manager): DCA consistently lowered maximum drawdowns in BTC-only portfolios, with overall performance equal to lump sum — [Amdax / Medium](https://medium.com/amdax-asset-management/lump-sum-or-dollar-cost-averaging-42c8f5bb9938); [Amdax research](https://www.amdax.com/en/research/guide/investing-in-bitcoin-which-strategy-works-best-2)
- Galaxy (Oct 2025): DCA had smaller drawdowns and fewer days underwater than lump sum — [Galaxy](https://www.galaxy.app/insights/the-benefits-of-dollar-cost-averaging-into-bitcoin)
- Coinbird (sponsored press release, May 2026): lump sum beat DCA at 1-, 2-, 3- and 4-year horizons in its scenarios. DCA won only across a full crash-and-recovery cycle, because it bought more during 2022. Headline claim: $100/month since 2015 ($13,700 invested) became about $632,000 — [The Block press release](https://www.theblock.co/press-releases/100-month-in-bitcoin-since-2015-would-have-turned-13700-into-632000-coinbird-analysis-shows); [Decrypt/Chainwire](https://decrypt.co/368329/100-month-in-bitcoin-since-2015-would-have-turned-13700-into-632000-coinbird-analysis-shows)
- BitcoinIRA backtest (cited by Spark.money): $500/month DCA over 2021–2025 ($24,000 total) grew to about $60,881 (+154%), vs +106% for lump sum over the same window. It is a single window that starts near a cycle top, so it favours DCA by construction — [Spark.money](https://www.spark.money/tools/bitcoin-accumulation-strategy-comparison)
- Spark.money cites dcabtc.com: monthly DCA was profitable in every rolling 3-year window since 2013 — [Spark.money](https://www.spark.money/tools/bitcoin-accumulation-strategy-comparison)
- Value averaging: a "Crypto Research Report" is cited as showing VA beat DCA in all ten years from 2010 to 2019, adding about 20% cumulative return. No methodology is given, and the original was not verified — [Spark.money](https://www.spark.money/tools/bitcoin-accumulation-strategy-comparison)
- Equity baseline: Vanguard (1926–2021) found lump sum beat DCA about 68% of the time, by about 2.3 pp on average — [Spark.money citing Vanguard](https://www.spark.money/tools/bitcoin-accumulation-strategy-comparison)
- Worst-case lump sum example: buying at the Nov 2021 peak (~$69k) meant being down 77% by June 2022 — [Spark.money](https://www.spark.money/tools/bitcoin-accumulation-strategy-comparison)
- Mayer Multiple = price / 200-day MA. Readings above about 2.4 are conventionally called "overheated"; that threshold is a convention, not a tested rule — [TheRightTrader](https://therighttrader.com/de/bitcoin-charts/mayer-multiple); [TradingView scripts](https://tradingview.com/script/TlNlaIid-Mayer-Multiple-Strategy)
- Search found no independent backtest of Mayer Multiple or 200-week-MA DCA. The available "evidence" is paid handbooks (e.g. a Payhip product testing 28 SMA variants) and TradingView indicators that explicitly do not backtest — [Payhip listing](https://payhip.com/b/0FwxA)

#### Inferences
- For someone paid monthly, "lump sum vs DCA" is mostly a false choice: new income gets invested when it arrives, which is DCA in practice. The real question is what to do with an existing cash pile, e.g. a bonus. Here the evidence (≈2/3 to 4/5 win rate for lump sum) favours deploying quickly, or over a short window of a few months, if a deep drawdown would be psychologically tolerable.
- Valuation-weighted DCA (buy more when Mayer Multiple < 1 or price is near the 200-week MA, less when MM > 2.4) is plausible but faces heavy hindsight and overfitting risk: only about 4 cycles of data, and the thresholds were chosen after the fact. A sensible, low-cost version is to keep the base DCA fixed and add a discretionary "dip budget". It should not be treated as alpha.
- Practical point backed by Bull Bitcoin's UTXO warning: buy often on the exchange, but withdraw rarely and in larger amounts (see custody section).

#### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed or academic 2024–2026 backtest of BTC DCA variants was found. The original Bull Bitcoin report and the "Crypto Research Report" VA study were not retrieved directly.
- Daily vs weekly vs monthly DCA frequency: no reliable comparison found (anecdotally negligible difference before fees).
- No PLN-denominated backtests found. FX (USD/PLN) effects for a Polish buyer are unquantified.

### Home mining in Poland (electricity, hashprice, heat, solar)

#### Takeaway
At 2026 Polish retail tariffs (~1.04–1.32 PLN/kWh gross all-in on G11), home mining is clearly unprofitable compared with buying BTC. #### Inferences
- Unit comparison (my calculation; the FX rate is assumed, not sourced). $138/MWh is $0.138/kWh. At roughly 3.6–3.8 PLN/USD that is about 0.50–0.52 PLN/kWh gross revenue for a top-efficiency ASIC, against a ~1.04–1.32 PLN/kWh cost. So mining yields well under half the sats that the same PLN would buy directly. Older or less efficient machines are worse, and this is before hardware depreciation, pool fees, noise and the next halving (~2028).
- Heating use case: resistive mining heat is 1 kWh of heat per kWh, but a heat pump delivers 3–4 kWh per kWh. Mining-as-heat only "wins" against direct electric heating, and only in heating months. Against a heat pump or gas, the offset is small.
- PV surplus: under net-billing, exported energy is valued at market prices (RCE), which are often low or even negative at midday in summer. Consuming surplus in an ASIC could beat exporting it at those hours. Whether that beats the ~0.50 PLN/kWh-equivalent revenue depends on RCE at the time, and solar is intermittent while ASICs are capex. This is a niche hobby case.
- A real non-financial benefit is sats without KYC history, which helps privacy. That may matter given the wrench-attack risk below.

Top tiers (e.g. 10%) generally require large token stakes or holdings on the exchange, which creates counterparty and altcoin risk that outweighs the cashback.
- Card cashback paid in BTC on an exchange is custodial. Withdraw periodically and in batches.

#### Gaps
- No primary-source verification of current tier conditions (Bybit, Bitpanda, Plutus). Fold, Lolli and Bitrefill availability in Poland was not confirmed.
- Tax treatment in Poland of crypto cashback was not researched.

### Yield products (CeFi earn, lending, wrapped-BTC DeFi)

#### Takeaway
The 2022 CeFi collapse is the core evidence. Celsius, BlockFi and FTX customers who chased a few % yield had their BTC frozen for 1–2+ years. Where recoveries happened, they were largely paid at Nov 2022 USD prices, not in BTC, so a bitcoiner lost most of the subsequent BTC upside. Earning ~1–5% yield does not compensate for a non-trivial chance of losing access to principal. That is why most bitcoiners avoid yield.

#### Cited Findings
- FTX's plan was approved in Delaware. Claims were valued at November 2022 prices, not current prices. Advisers said repurchasing crypto to repay in kind would be "exorbitantly expensive". FTX said it expected 100% plus interest for non-government creditors in USD terms, while critics noted FTX.com held only 0.1% of the bitcoin customers thought they had — [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/cefi/ftx-s-bankruptcy-plan-approved-clearing-path-for-98-customer-repayments); [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/284982); [NYSSCPA](https://nysscpa.org/news/1046014-customers-of-bankrupt-ftx-to-get-all-their-money-back-2024-05-08)
- BlockFi exited bankruptcy with a liquidation plan. Some creditors received partial repayment in BTC/ETH, and unsecured creditors were estimated to get roughly 35%–63% (snippet partially truncated) — [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/ip-law/blockfi-cleared-to-repay-customers-through-liquidation-plan); [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/203099/blockfi-ends-bankruptcy-prepares-to-return-customer-funds?amp=1)
- Celsius began distributions after its plan, including equity in Ionic Digital, a creditor-owned mining company — [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/celsius-begins-asset-distributions-to-customers-as-court-approves-ftx-s-repayment-plan)

#### Inferences
- "100% recovery" headlines (FTX) are in USD at the trough price. In BTC terms, a holder whose BTC was valued at ~$16–17k in Nov 2022 and repaid in cash in 2025 recovered only a fraction of their BTC. This is the key point for a sats-maximiser.
- Wrapped BTC (WBTC, cbBTC, etc.) and DeFi lending add bridge/custodian, smart-contract, oracle and liquidation risks on top. The "yield" is compensation for those risks, not free money.
- Structural rule: BTC that earns yield is BTC someone else controls. That contradicts the self-custody objective.

#### Gaps
- Exact Celsius BTC in-kind recovery percentage was not found, and court/claims-agent documents were not retrieved.

### Leverage, altcoins, emergency fund, allocation sizing, behavioural pitfalls

#### Takeaway
BTC has had four drawdowns of roughly 75–93% (2011, 2014–15, 2018, 2022). Any leverage would have been liquidated in each one, and any holder who needed to sell during one locked in heavy losses. The biggest practical risks to sats accumulation are being forced or panicked into selling, plus leverage and altcoin detours. A cash emergency fund, and a position size you can hold through -75%, are the protection.

#### Cited Findings
- Historical peak-to-trough drawdowns: 2011 about 93%; 2014–15 about 82–86%; 2017–18 about 83–84% (to ~$3,250); 2021–22 about 68–77%, with Glassnode at ~76%. K33 notes the 2014 and 2018 bottoms came 12–13 months after the declines began — [Bitcoin Education substack](https://bitcoineducation.substack.com/p/when-will-the-bitcoin-price-bottom); [K33](https://k33.com/research/archive/articles/the-2022-bear-market-is-on-track-with-the-2018-bear-market); [Cryptoast](https://cryptoast.fr/?p=225871); [TradingView BTC ATH drawdown](https://www.tradingview.com/symbols/BTC_ATHDRAWDOWN/ideas)
- NYDIG: four major cycles (peaks in 2011, 2013, 2017, 2021), each followed by 75–90% drawdowns. Sizeable drawdowns also happen inside bull markets — [NYDIG](https://nydig.com/research/charting-drawdowns-during-up-cycles); [Blockworks](https://blockworks.co/news/bitcoin-bull-market-drawdowns)
- Lump sum at the Nov 2021 top was -77% by June 2022 — [Spark.money](https://www.spark.money/tools/bitcoin-accumulation-strategy-comparison)
- 2026 context: BTC is at ~$83k and -4.5% YTD at end-Sep 2026, with mining at near-breakeven hashprice — [Hashrate Index](https://hashrateindex.com/blog/hashrate-index-roundup-september-28-2026/)

#### Inferences
- Leverage: at 2x leverage a -50% move liquidates the position, and every past cycle exceeded that. Futures and perps also carry funding costs. For long-horizon accumulation, leverage has a negative expected value in sats once liquidation risk and fees are counted.
- Altcoins: altcoin purchases are typically priced and judged in BTC terms, and most underperform BTC over cycles. That is opinion common in bitcoiner literature; I did not source quantitative data in this pass.
- Emergency fund: a B2B/JDG worker has no paid sick leave or notice-period protection, and ZUS/health contributions keep running. A larger cash buffer (e.g. 6–12 months of expenses plus tax/ZUS reserves) than a UoP employee would need is prudent. This is my inference; I found no Polish-specific source.
- Sizing heuristic: choose an allocation where a 75–80% drawdown would not change your lifestyle or force a sale, and where the remaining assets (cash, IKE/IKZE, bonds) cover medium-term goals. The 2022 drawdown lasted about a year to bottom, and multi-year recovery is the base case.
- Behavioural guardrails: automate recurring buys, avoid checking price daily, pre-commit in writing to "no selling below X months of holding", and keep keys offline so impulsive selling has friction.

#### Gaps
- No academic study was found on retail behaviour (selling in drawdowns) specifically for BTC holders in 2024–2026.
- No Polish-specific emergency-fund guidance for JDG was sourced.

### Self-custody best practices (hardware wallets, multisig, backups, UTXO hygiene, inheritance, wrench-attack opsec)

#### Takeaway
2026 delivered a concrete lesson: the Coldcard RNG flaw (disclosed 30 Jul 2026). Seeds generated on affected Coldcard firmware since 2021 had about 40–72 bits of entropy, and over 1,000 BTC was drained. Firmware updates do not fix existing seeds. Good practice: multisig across vendors (e.g. Trezor + BitBox02 + Jade), or at least added user entropy (dice) and a strong passphrase. Also steel backups, an inheritance plan, rare batched withdrawals with UTXO consolidation in low-fee periods, and strict privacy. Europe, and France especially, became the global hotspot for crypto "wrench attacks" in 2025.

#### Cited Findings
**Coldcard RNG incident (July 2026)**
- Coinkite disclosed on 30 Jul 2026 that a 2021 firmware change made seed generation use a weak software generator instead of the hardware RNG. A config flag was checked for presence rather than enabled state — [Forklog](https://forklog.com/en/kraken-cso-says-coldcard-flaw-exposes-gap-in-hardware-wallet-testing/)
- Effective entropy: about 40 bits for Mk2/Mk3 seeds and about 72 bits for Mk4/Mk5/Q, vs 128 expected — [Forklog](https://forklog.com/en/?p=101360)
- Affected firmware: Mk2/Mk3 4.0.1–4.1.9; Mk4/Mk5 Standard < 5.6.0, Edge < 6.6.0X; Q Standard < 1.5.0Q, Edge < 6.6.0QX — [Noqta](https://noqta.tn/en/news/coldcard-rng-firmware-flaw-bitcoin-theft-2026)
- Hotfixes shipped 31 Jul. Existing seeds are not repaired: generate a new seed on fixed firmware and move funds. A strong unique BIP-39 passphrase, or ≥50 dice rolls, mitigates the risk — [Casa](https://blog.casa.io/coldcard-vulnerability/)
- Galaxy Research counted 1,196 addresses drained for about 1,082.65 BTC on 30 Jul, with further waves after. Reported USD totals conflict ($38M vs >$90M) — [Forklog](https://forklog.com/en/kraken-cso-says-coldcard-flaw-exposes-gap-in-hardware-wallet-testing/?hl=en-US); [Bitcoinwell](https://bitcoinwell.com/blog/the-coldcard-hack-a-timeline-of-the-dollar38m-entropy-failure-and-how-to-keep-it-from-happening-to-you)
- Kraken's CSO: audits can confirm an approved RNG exists but not that production firmware actually uses it — [Forklog](https://forklog.com/en/kraken-cso-says-coldcard-flaw-exposes-gap-in-hardware-wallet-testing/)
- Comparison sources argue that multisig across vendors removes single-vendor failure — [onrampbitcoin comparison 2026](https://onrampbitcoin.com/knowledge-center/hardware-wallet-comparison-2026)

**Device notes**
- Trezor (SatoshiLabs, Czech) Safe 7 has the TROPIC01 open secure element plus an EAL6+ chip and combines four entropy sources (vendor FAQ claim). Trezor stores no passphrase, so losing the seed backup or the passphrase means losing that wallet — [Bitbo comparison](https://bitbo.io/tools/trezor-safe-7-vs-bitbox02/); [onrampbitcoin](https://onrampbitcoin.com/knowledge-center/hardware-wallet-comparison-2026)

**UTXO / fee hygiene**
- Small, frequent on-chain DCA withdrawals create UTXOs that are expensive to consolidate later — [Spark.money summarising Bull Bitcoin](https://www.spark.money/tools/bitcoin-dca-backtest-calculator)
- Fees were only 0.70% of block rewards in late Sept 2026, i.e. a low-fee environment — [Hashrate Index](https://hashrateindex.com/blog/hashrate-index-roundup-september-28-2026/)

#### Inferences
- Practical stack for a technical user. Standard practices, with the Coldcard incident as evidence:
  - (a) Use a 2-of-3 multisig with devices from different vendors (e.g. Trezor + BitBox02 + Jade), coordinated in Sparrow or Electrum, with the output descriptor backed up in every seed location.
  - (b) Or a single-sig hardware wallet with a strong BIP-39 passphrase, and user-added dice entropy when the device supports it.
  - (c) Stamp seeds on steel, stored in geographically separated places.
  - (d) Test recovery with a small amount before funding.
  - (e) Keep firmware current, and read security advisories.
- Anyone who generated a seed on a Coldcard before the July 2026 fixed firmware should treat that seed as compromised: create a new one on fixed firmware or another vendor's device, then sweep the funds.
- Withdrawal batching: accumulate on the exchange and withdraw in larger chunks (e.g. monthly or quarterly, or once above a size threshold). Use a fresh receive address every time (no address reuse). Consolidate small UTXOs only during low-fee periods such as late 2026, ideally within one wallet/account to limit privacy linkage.
- Wrench-attack opsec:
  - Don't disclose holdings publicly (social media, conference badges, LinkedIn "bitcoin investor").
  - Use multisig with a geographically distant key, or a timelocked or collaborative-custody setup, so funds cannot be moved under duress at home.
  - Keep only a small hot/decoy wallet.
  - Keep exchange KYC data and delivery addresses for hardware purchases minimal (data leaks have fed past targeting).
  - Note that Polish KNF/CEIDG registries expose JDG addresses: consider a virtual office address for the JDG.
- Inheritance: leave a written, non-technical recovery letter. Name who holds which key, the wallet software, and the descriptor. Avoid putting the seed and passphrase in the same place or in the will. Consider a collaborative-custody provider or an informed technical executor.
